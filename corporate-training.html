Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

Can the programme be customised for our team?

Yes. We plan the curriculum around your software, current skill level and project needs.

Where is the training delivered?

At our Kumbakonam training centre or online, depending on your requirement. Tell us what suits your team and we will suggest the best option.

Do participants receive certificates?

Yes. Participants who complete the programme and pass the assessment receive a Cadpoint course completion certificate.

How do I get a proposal?

Share your team size, software and goals on WhatsApp or call us, and we will send a suitable programme plan.

Planning training for your team?

Share your requirement and we will get back with a suitable programme.
