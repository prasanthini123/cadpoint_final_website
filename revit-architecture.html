Revit Architecture – BIM Modeling & Documentation

CTrainer: Cadpoint Expert TrainersCategory: Civil DesignDuration: 100 hours

What Will You Learn?

Revit is the leading BIM (Building Information Modeling) software used by architects and civil engineers. Instead of drawing lines, you build an intelligent 3D building model and generate plans, elevations, sections and schedules from it automatically.

In this course you will model a complete building from scratch, create families, prepare quantity schedules and sheets, and present your project with renders.

  • Model walls, floors, roofs, stairs, doors and windows
  • Create custom families and components
  • Generate plans, sections, elevations and schedules automatically
  • Sheet setup, annotation, plotting and rendering

Course Curriculum

8 stages · 100 hours · theory with lab practice

01Interface & Project Setup6 hrs

Revit workspace, templates, units, levels and grids.

02Walls, Doors, Windows & Floors14 hrs

Building the basic structure of a project with architectural elements.

03Roofs, Stairs & Railings12 hrs

Roof types, staircases, railings, ramps and openings.

04Families & Components14 hrs

Loadable families, parameters, in-place families and furniture.

05Massing, Site & Topography10 hrs

Conceptual massing, toposurface, site plan and building pad.

06Views, Schedules & Quantities14 hrs

Plans, sections, elevations, 3D views, schedules and material take-off.

07Annotation, Sheets & Plotting10 hrs

Dimensions, tags, text, sheet composition, print and PDF.

08Rendering & Final Project20 hrs

Materials, lighting, rendering and a complete building project with assessment.

About your trainer

Your Revit Architecture trainer teaches with the drawings, models and workflows used in civil and architectural offices. Every concept is shown on screen first, then practised by you on your own system, so each session ends with something you built.

Expect drawing standards, layer and file discipline from the first class: the habits that make your work easy for a site engineer or senior designer to open, read and use.

What you will be taught
  • Interface & Project Setup
  • Walls, Doors, Windows & Floors
  • Roofs, Stairs & Railings
  • Families & Components
  • Massing, Site & Topography
  • Views, Schedules & Quantities
By the end of the course you will
  • Produce clear, standard-compliant drawings and models
  • Organise layers, blocks and sheets the way offices do
  • Finish a project you can show in your portfolio
